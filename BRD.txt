2. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

NaturaAI is an AI-powered platform that predicts and recommends herbal remedies
for common ailments. It analyzes user-selected herbs and generates a verdict
(SAFE / CAUTION / UNSAFE) backed by a deterministic ML ensemble, curated
knowledge, and — optionally — a grounded large-language-model (LLM) assistant.

This document defines the BUSINESS requirements: the rationale, market
opportunity, stakeholders, business goals, measurable success criteria,
financial model, constraints, risks, and the go-to-market approach. It is the
business contract that the PRD (functional/technical requirements) must satisfy.

Core positioning: a credible, transparent, data-driven digital health-and-wellness
education product — NOT a diagnostic or medical-treatment tool. All content is
educational and carries prominent medical disclaimers.

Key business targets (v1.0):
  - Launch with a validated, defensible knowledge base:
    368 herbs, 39 curated combinations, 15 disease mappings.
  - Live free-tier MVP on render/Vercel/Neon within a low-cost budget
    (target < $50/month operating cost at launch).
  - Establish a monetization path (freemium → subscription; later B2B)
    without compromising the educational-first trust model.

3. BUSINESS CONTEXT & BACKGROUND
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3.1 Market Problem
    - Consumers increasingly seek natural/alternative remedies for everyday
      ailments, but information sources are fragmented: forums, folklore,
      blogs, and unverified social-media claims.
    - Little to no structure exists for comparing herb effectiveness,
      safety, toxicity, and drug-interaction risk in one place.
    - Mainstream consumers are not equipped to weigh toxicity risk,
      recommended dosage, and preparation method, leading to unsafe self-care.
    - The market opportunity is disproportionately attractive because
      complementary-and-alternative-medicine (CAM) is growing globally while
      regulation remains light — creating demand for a trust anchor.

3.2 Why Now
    - Generative AI has lowered the cost of building a grounded assistant
      that cites sources and explains verdicts.
    - The project already possesses a validated ML artifact (ensemble model:
      Random Forest / Gradient Boosting / XGBoost / LightGBM) trained on a
      permissively licensed dataset (Amidha, CC-BY-4.0).
    - Infrastructure (Next.js frontend, Spring Boot backend, FastAPI ML engine,
      Postgres/Redis/RabbitMQ/Qdrant) is already built and deployed on
      free/low-cost tiers.

3.3 Constraints We Must Not Violate
    - NaturaAI is an EDUCATION product. It must never diagnose or treat.
    - All claims must trace to a source (curated data / citations).
    - The assistant must be "retrieval-first": grounded in the local knowledge
      base, not free-generation.
    - Free-tier hosting imposes cost ceilings and availability expectations.

4. MARKET OPPORTUNITY & TARGET SEGMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

4.1 Market Sizing (indicative, directional)
    - The global herbal medicine / CAM market is valued in the tens of billions
      USD and is growing at high single-digit CAGR.
    - Wellness apps are among the fastest-growing consumer app categories;
      consumers are willing to pay for credible, personalized wellness insight.
    - Within this, "herb & supplement insight" tools remain under-served by
      trustworthy, AI-driven products.

4.2 Target Segments (ordered by priority)
    S1  Wellness-curious consumers (25-55) seeking safe, evidence-minded
        guidance on common ailments (sleep, stress, digestion, immunity).
    S2  Lifestyle-trackers / self-quantifiers who want reproducible,
        data-backed wellness decisions.
    S3  Informed self-carers already using supplements who need
        interaction/duplication warnings (combinations, allergens).
    S4  (Future / v2.0) B2B: wellness clinics, telehealth providers,
        supplement e-commerce — white-label "check my stack" tools.

4.3 Segment Priorities for v1.0
    v1.0 targets S1 and S2 with S3 as a differentiator (safety features).
    S4 is deferred to v2.0 and described only as an opportunity.

5. VALUE PROPOSITION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

For wellness-curious consumers, NaturaAI is the credible, transparent way to
understand herbal remedies, because it turns scattered folklore into
reproducible, source-backed verdicts — unlike forums and blogs.

    Differentiators
    - Deterministic, explainable scoring (compatibility, safety, benefit, risk,
      confidence) instead of opaque advice.
    - Explicit safety signals: toxicity, preparation, dosage, frequency,
      interaction warnings.
    - Grounded assistant that cites the knowledge base before it answers.
    - Privacy-forward: account optional for browsing; JWT + OAuth2 auth;
      data stored in our Postgres/Redis/Qdrant stack.
    - Educational-first trust model with prominent disclaimers.

7. BUSINESS GOALS & MEASURABLE KPIs
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

7.1 Strategic Goals (G)
    G1  Launch a trustworthy, educational herbal-insight product that users
        return to.
    G2  Establish a defensible data + AI moat (curated knowledge base, trained
        ensemble, retrieval-first assistant).
    G3  Prove a sustainable unit economics model before scaling spend.
    G4  Lay a compliance-safe foundation (education-first) so future
        monetization and B2B expansion do not create regulatory surprises.

7.2 KPIs & Targets (v1.0 launch, first 90 days)
    KPI                             Target (90d)
    ----------------------------    ---------------------------
    K1  Active users (weekly)       >= 500 WAU at 90d
    K2  Analyses completed          >= 2,000 total
    K3  Completion rate             >= 40% of started analyses
    K4  Return rate                 >= 20% WAU/MAU ratio
    K5  Assistant usage             >= 30% of active users try it
    K6  Retention (d30)             >= 15%
    K7  Safety-flag exposure        >= 90% of UNSAFE/CAUTION verdicts
                                       display a clear warning (QA-gated)
    K8  Uptime                      >= 99.5% (free-tier realistic)
    K9  Monthly infra cost          < $50/month at launch scale
    K10 NPS                          >= 30 (survey on d7)
    K11 Zero critical PII breaches  = 0 (audit-gated)

7.3 KPI Ownership & Cadence
    Product owns K1-K7; Ops owns K8-K9; Legal/Trust owns K11.
    Review cadence: weekly (ops cost), monthly (product KPIs), quarterly
    (strategy, roadmap, monetization go/no-go).

8. BUSINESS REQUIREMENTS (BR)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Legend: M = Must (v1.0), S = Should (v1.x), W = Won't (v2.0 / out of scope).

    BR-1  [M] Educational positioning
          All user-facing output must be presented as educational information,
          not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. A prominent disclaimer
          must be visible on analysis results, assistant responses, and the
          plant scan feature.

    BR-2  [M] Credible knowledge base
          Launch with >= 300 herbs, >= 35 curated combinations, and >= 12
          disease mappings, all traceable to the Amidha (CC-BY-4.0) dataset
          and clearly attributed. Any manually curated data must be versioned
          and reviewed before publishing.

    BR-3  [M] Explainable, deterministic verdicts
          Every analysis must produce a verdict (SAFE / CAUTION / UNSAFE) with
          the five scores (compatibility, safety, benefit, risk, confidence),
          plus toxicity, preparation, dosage, and frequency guidance — so a
          business user (non-technical) can explain the outcome.

    BR-4  [M] Safety-first experience
          UNSAFE / CAUTION results must surface a clear warning to the user and
          recommend consulting a healthcare professional. The system must flag
          allergen and duplicate-combination risks.

    BR-5  [M] Grounded AI assistant
          Assistant answers must be retrieval-first, grounded in the local
          knowledge base, cite sources where available, and degrade gracefully
          when the knowledge base lacks an answer (no fabricated content).

    BR-6  [M] Low-cost sustainable operations
          The v1.0 platform must run on free/low-cost tiers (Render, Vercel,
          Neon, managed Redis/RabbitMQ/Qdrant) with a documented cost ceiling
          and observability so spend is predictable and bounded.

    BR-7  [M] Privacy & security baseline
          Account-based features must use secure auth (JWT + OAuth2 with Google
          sign-in), email verification and password reset, encryption of
          secrets, and no PII logging. Compliance with applicable data
          protection rules (e.g., GDPR principles) must be documented.

    BR-8  [M] Instrumentation & measurement
          The platform must measure the KPIs in section 7.2 (analytics events,
          dashboards) so business value can be proven before scaling spend.

    BR-9  [M] Quality gate for release
          The build must pass CI on every change (backend Maven tests, frontend
          Vitest + ESLint + build, ML pytest) before anything ships, to protect
          the trust model.

    BR-10 [S] Plant scan (v1.x, heuristic v1)
          A basic plant-photo recognition result (v1 heuristic stub) that
          explicitly frames output as an unverified suggestion with a clear
          educational disclaimer. Not promoted as a diagnostic capability.

    BR-11 [S] Personalization (v1.x)
          Optional user profile with allergies/avoidances to personalize
          recommendations, surfaced transparently as opt-in.

    BR-12 [S] Monetization design (v1.x, not before trust metrics are met)
          Freemium design (free analyses + gated premium features) only after
          K1, K2, K4, and K11 targets are demonstrated. No paywall before
          trust/retention proof points.

    BR-13 [W] B2B / white-label (v2.0)
          Deferred. Opportunity documented; no commitments in v1.0.

    BR-14 [W] Regulatory-approved medical claims (v2.0+)
          Deferred. v1.0 explicitly does not make medical claims.

    BR-15 [M] Accessibility & inclusivity
          The experience must meet WCAG AA-level accessibility on core journeys
          (analysis, results, assistant) to serve diverse consumers and avoid
          exclusion risk.

    BR-16 [M] Content moderation / disclaimer enforcement
          All generated or curated content must pass a disclaimer/red-flag
          check before being served (QA + automated check in CI).

9. CONSTRAINTS, ASSUMPTIONS & DEPENDENCIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

9.1 Constraints
    C1  Education-only positioning; no medical claims (BR-1, BR-14).
    C2  Free/low-cost infrastructure budget at launch (BR-6).
    C3  Dataset license (CC-BY-4.0) must be honored and attributed.
    C4  Single-owner maintenance reality: changes must remain reviewable and
        low-complexity to sustain.
    C5  Privacy and security practices must not regress across releases.

9.2 Assumptions
    A1  Consumers accept "educational guidance" with clear disclaimers.
    A2  Free tier (Render/Vercel/Neon) is sufficient for launch traffic;
        scaling triggers the cost review in BR-6.
    A3  Amidha dataset accuracy is adequate for educational use and clearly
        attributed; no clinical validation required at v1.0.
    A4  LLM provider optionality (OpenAI / Llama / Gemma / Mistral) keeps
        assistant costs bounded; default is retrieval-first and can run
        without an external LLM.
    A5  Marketing is low-budget (content, SEO, community) at launch.

9.3 Dependencies
    D1  ML engine model artifact (predictor.joblib) and curated CSV datasets.
    D2  Live backend on Render and database on Neon (already deployed).
    D3  CI pipelines on GitHub Actions (already green on main).
    D4  Frontend build chain (Next 16 / React 19 / Tailwind 4).

    (K1 >= 500 WAU, K4 >= 20%, K6 >= 15%, K11 = 0) all hold.

12. SUCCESS CRITERIA (Definition of Business Done)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

v1.0 launch is a business success when ALL of the following hold by day 90:
    - K1 >= 500 WAU, K2 >= 2,000 analyses, K3 >= 40% completion,
      K4 >= 20% return ratio, K5 >= 30% assistant try-rate.
    - K6 >= 15% d30 retention, K7 >= 90% safety-flag coverage,
      K8 >= 99.5% uptime, K9 < $50/month, K11 = 0.
    - BR-1..BR-9 fully implemented and evidenced (QA + CI green).
    - No medical-claim or data-privacy incident logged.
